## Choosing the evidence

- **Full-stack and application development:** Lead with Buildplate's application features and onboarding improvements. Follow with ForgeX's back-end development, historical-data ingestion, migration, and developer handoff. Mention CPEN 221 when Java or software construction is relevant.
- **Data engineering and information management:** Lead with cell-safe's database schema and Python/SQL/Spark pipeline. Follow with ForgeX's batched ingestion and incremental synchronization. Discuss how data structures support downstream questions and applications.
- **Data science, NLP, and generative AI:** Combine cell-safe with the Vancouver AI Data Storytelling Hackathon and PlatoAI. Explain the analytical question, methods, and how the interface or citations helped users explore the results.
- **Project management and documentation:** Use Handy Andy to discuss project planning, a charter, risk tracking, progress reports, cost tracking, and lessons learned. State your own contribution accurately. Claim PMBOK familiarity only if the course explicitly taught or followed it.
- **Collaboration:** Use a specific action, such as refining cell-safe's scenario after Professor Gopaluni's snowstorm example or supporting ForgeX's developer handoff. Explain what changed because of the interaction.

## Sources and accuracy

- Start with the current [résumé](resume.md), the actual posting, and relevant material in [linkedin_export](linkedin_export/). Check [GitHub](https://github.com/Toricane) when implementation details would improve the letter. Reconcile older posts with current project documentation.
- Use only technologies, responsibilities, metrics, and outcomes supported by those sources or confirmed directly. Ask about missing facts when they materially affect the letter; do not fill gaps with plausible achievements.
- Distinguish your individual contributions from the team's work. Preserve the actual startup, founder, or collaboration arrangement; do not imply paid employment or a formal internship without confirmation.
- Describe cell-safe as an emergency-response planning prototype using synthetic data. Active sessions are not verified unique people; home origins do not establish destinations or assistance needs. Do not claim a live population feed, validated emergency outcomes, or forecasting.
- Do not infer Azure experience from Databricks, forecasting from historical-data processing, or agentic AI from generative AI alone.
- Current coursework is evidence of learning, not completed training or professional experience. Confirm specific testing or QA work before claiming it.
- The year of study and January–April 2027 availability reflect the current application cycle. Update them for future terms. Never imply eight-month availability when only four months are possible.

## Final review

- Confirm the employer, role, recipient, requisition ID, date, and availability in the opening, subject, and closing. Search for names left over from another application.
- Use the employer's requisition ID accurately; label a separate university identifier “UBC posting ID.” Omit the personal UBC student number unless requested.
- Prefer concrete decisions and outcomes to lists of technologies or personality traits. Tailor all three evidence paragraphs to this posting.
- Check eligibility requirements separately, including degree level and work-term duration. Do not write around a mismatch by implying qualifications you do not have.
- Export and inspect the complete PDF. Keep it one page with readable text, clear paragraph spacing, and footer clearance. Rephrase isolated final words or very short final lines instead of shrinking the font or adding filler.
- Follow the posting's document and submission instructions, including the co-op footer where required.
